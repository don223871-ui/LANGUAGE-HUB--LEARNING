# LISTENING · COMPLETE MICRO-SKILLS MASTER

## 01 · Listening Foundations
### 01.1 Sound Discrimination
- **Phoneme discrimination** — distinguish similar speech sounds.
- **Vowel discrimination** — distinguish vowel contrasts in connected speech.
- **Consonant discrimination** — identify consonant contrasts and substitutions.
- **Minimal-pair perception** — hear meaning-changing sound differences.
- **Sound-sequence recognition** — recognise recurring sound patterns.
### 01.2 Word Recognition in Speech
- **Whole-word recognition** — recognise familiar words rapidly.
- **Syllable recognition** — identify syllable boundaries and patterns.
- **Stress-based recognition** — use word stress to identify words.
- **Reduced-word recognition** — recognise weak and reduced forms.
- **Lexical segmentation** — separate words from continuous speech.
### 01.3 Speech Segmentation
- **Word-boundary detection** — locate likely word boundaries.
- **Chunk segmentation** — divide speech into meaningful groups.
- **Phrase-boundary perception** — detect boundaries between phrases.
- **Pause interpretation** — use pauses to structure incoming speech.
- **Continuous-speech tracking** — maintain the stream without losing position.
### 01.4 Prosodic Awareness
- **Stress perception** — identify prominent syllables and words.
- **Rhythm perception** — recognise stressed and unstressed patterns.
- **Intonation perception** — track pitch movement and contour.
- **Pausing and phrasing** — use timing to interpret structure.
- **Prominence detection** — identify information made salient by the speaker.
